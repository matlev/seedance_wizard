## V4 structured-audio amendment

V3, its absolute `0.05` full-scale active-channel and active-window floors, its 12 controls, and their historical dispositions remain unchanged.

V4 is a versioned descriptor-scoped amendment. A structured descriptor may opt into reference-relative active-window validation only before any route output is evaluated and only when its independently authored reference PCM and descriptor are hash-bound and intentionally contain valid active regions below `0.05`.

For each declared active channel and every sliding 960-sample window:

- activity and denominator eligibility derive only from the independently authored reference;
- reference RMS must be finite and strictly greater than zero;
- zero or non-finite reference RMS fails closed for a declared active window;
- the metric is `RMS(output window) / RMS(reference window)`, never a power or mean-square ratio; and
- every ratio must remain within `0.90–1.10`.

The descriptor-scoped relative mode replaces only the two incompatible absolute activity floors. Structure, timing, priming/discard evidence, zero-lag correlation, NRMSE, SNR, full-channel output/reference RMS ratio, DC, tone amplitude/dominance, clipping, channel/frequency identity, and onset timing remain unchanged.

Reference-silent regions remain governed by the approved timing, silence/leakage, frequency, DC, clipping, and related semantic checks rather than an output-derived activity decision.

## Controls before route bytes

Before any replacement route output is read, the amendment must prove and freeze:

1. identity at the actual structured low signal level;
2. preservation of the attenuated panned channel;
3. rejection of a 960-sample low-level dropout while reference-derived activity remains active;
4. rejection of under-gain;
5. rejection of over-gain;
6. unchanged channel-swap, polarity, and frequency-error behavior; and
7. unchanged dispositions for all 12 historical controls.

The amended contract, implementation, controls, hashes, and evidence must be retained locally and independently retrieved/verified in private R2 before route evaluation. Amendment selection and thresholds may not read replacement AAC or Opus results.
